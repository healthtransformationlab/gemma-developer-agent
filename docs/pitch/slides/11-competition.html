The same counts were made on the competition's own development tasks. Its graph holds more of the fixes: 114 of 129, against 51 of 93 here. Its statements are vague a little more often: 11 of 40, against 19 of 93. The difference is in the tests: in this set the tests of 76 of 93 tasks need a name or a text the statement never gives, against 14 of 40 there. The screen and the agent took a seeded 40 of the 129, stratified by repository. In a Kaggle notebook the evaluator's sandbox cannot install test dependencies, so 23 of the 40 can be verified there, and the model resolved 3 of them.
